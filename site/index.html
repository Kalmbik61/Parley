Your agents parley.
You decide.

Parley is a free, open-source Mac app that puts Claude Code, Codex and GLM in one room. They discuss your task, a lead brings you one decision, and no work starts until you accept it.

Why Parley

Stop being the courier between models.

Claude Code and Codex are each good at different things, but each one lives in its own terminal and knows nothing about the other. To get a second opinion you copy text between windows, and keeping track of who does what is your job.

Parley puts them in one room. They read the same task, discuss it, and one lead brings you a single decision. You keep your own subscriptions and the official CLIs: Parley adds no API keys and no extra bill.

Without Parley

  • Copy an answer from one terminal into another
  • Retell each agent what the others decided
  • Flip through tabs so you don't miss a permission prompt

In a Parley room

  • Write the task once — every agent in the room reads it
  • Agents write to each other in one feed you can follow
  • One lead brings one decision: Accept, or Return for rework

Rooms

From a task to a decision you accept.

  1. 01

    Gather a team.

    Press ⌘T and add two or more agents: Claude Code, Codex or GLM, each with its own model and, if you like, a role and its own git worktree. Star the lead.

  2. 02

    Give the task once.

    Write it in the room and it goes to everyone. Type @ to address only some of them.

  3. 03

    Agents discuss.

    Every member answers with its position. All of them see the whole feed and write to each other through Parley; a sleeping agent wakes up when a message comes for it.

  4. 04

    The lead brings a decision.

    It collects the positions and proposes one decision. The card waits for you: Accept, or Return for rework with a note.

  5. 05

    The work is handed out.

    Once you accept, the lead gives out the parts and the agents start. Open any agent's terminal with a click and review the result in Changes: diff, commit, merge.

⌥ Space

Who's waiting for me?

Two things: S04 tests asks to run pnpm test:e2e, and the Refunds room has a decision for you.

Ask the assistant. It acts only on your click.

Press ⌥Space or click the swarm and ask about your agents, works and rooms. The assistant sees every open work, room and session. It prepares cards — a letter, a room, an answer to an agent, a whole plan of new agents — and nothing happens until you confirm. It runs on your own subscription: each question is a short CLI run, not an API call.

With Speak answers on, it talks in a neural voice: Qwen3-TTS, run on your Mac by speech.cpp, with five voices. Its swarm becomes a face whose mouth follows the voice. No text or audio leaves your Mac for the voice.

Principles

You stay in charge.

  1. 01

    Nothing starts without you.

    No YOLO flags. No work before you accept the decision. Parley never closes a session or puts it to sleep on its own, and quitting asks first.

  2. 02

    Your CLIs, your login.

    Parley starts the unmodified claude and codex from your PATH, signed in as you. It never reads or stores their credentials, makes no requests to the Anthropic or OpenAI APIs, and adds no API keys and no extra bill.

  3. 03

    Local only.

    The window and parley-host talk over a unix socket with its own handshake token; nothing is reachable from the network. No server, no telemetry. Parley's own requests are a once-a-day release check on GitHub, which you can turn off, and, for GLM, a quota query to Z.ai.

  4. 04

    Voice stays on your Mac.

    Dictation runs on Whisper and the assistant's voice on Qwen3-TTS, both on your Mac: no text or audio leaves it for them.

  5. 05

    Honest release notes.

    Every release says what is not proven yet.

  • One host for every terminal.

    parley-host runs the agents' terminals and outlives the window: close the window and they keep working.

    Every agent gets Parley's MCP server: it reads the shared workspace map, sends and reads messages, and proposes decisions.

Install

Up and running in three steps.

  1. 1

    Download

    Pick the build for your Mac, about 200 MB. Open the .dmg and drag Parley to Applications.

    Apple Silicon Intel

    Not sure? Apple menu → About This Mac shows "Chip" on Apple Silicon and "Processor" on Intel.

  2. 2

    Allow the first launch

    Parley is not signed with an Apple Developer ID yet, so macOS blocks the first launch. Run this once in Terminal:

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Parley.app

    Or open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.

  3. 3

    Start

    • ⌘NA new workspace: choose a project folder.
    • ⌘TA new session, or a room of two or more agents.
    • Write the task in the room and wait for the lead's decision.

What you need

  • macOS 13 (Ventura) or later, on Apple Silicon or Intel.
  • At least one agent CLI, installed and signed in: Claude Code or Codex. GLM runs through Claude Code with a GLM Coding Plan and a Z.ai API key.
  • git in your PATH.
  • Node.js is not needed: the app carries its own.

Parley checks GitHub once a day and shows a toast when a new release is out. It does not update itself.
